# /pipeline

The Monday review: every live deal by stage, its last real touch, the next move with a date, anything quiet for 7+ days, and the forecast against the quarter's target. Fix the CRM as you go (notes, stages on evidence). Output: a one-screen table, then the three deals to move this week.

Finish every run with one line in `memory/log.md` and, in a scheduled run, your contribution in `../../team/inbox/<date>/ella.md`.
