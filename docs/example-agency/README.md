# An agency example: from a signed deal to the first invoice

The same made-up studio as [the example day](../example-day/). Northwind's board said yes on Monday 16 March, and
this is what moves between the interns in the four days after, with nobody chasing anybody. Every name, email and
figure is invented.

1. **Signed (Tue 17 Mar).** Sales (Ella) writes [the handover](inbox/2026-03-17/handover-northwind.md) for the head
   intern: scope, dates, who's who, what was promised on the calls. In her
   [contribution](inbox/2026-03-17/ella.md) she flags the payment terms for finance.
2. **Delivery starts.** The head intern (Theo) turns the handover into [delivery notes](delivery-notes-northwind.md):
   the plan by week, the first milestone, what Alex needs to do and when. Delivery is the head intern's job, done
   with you.
3. **First invoice (Fri 20 Mar).** Finance (Arthur) reads the terms from the handoff and drafts
   [invoice 1048](inbox/2026-03-20/invoice-draft-1048.md) with a card for Alex to sign off. Nothing is sent until Alex
   does.
4. **Paid (Sun 5 Apr).** The payment shows up in the next bank statement (it's in
   [the sample statement](../../agents/arthur/tools/sample.csv)), and finance records it as paid in the ledger.

The rules behind it are the handoffs in [team/TEAM.md](../../team/TEAM.md): a deal signed goes from sales to the
head intern, a proposal accepted goes from sales to finance, and a delivery milestone goes from the head intern to
finance.
